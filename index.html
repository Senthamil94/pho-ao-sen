Our story

A lotus that bloomed
in muddy water.

Ao Sen means lotus pond — a Vietnamese symbol of strength and enlightenment. Just as a lotus blooms out of muddy water, our family's story grew out of one of the hardest chapters in Vietnamese history.

Our family immigrated to America during the Vietnam War and brought the culinary traditions of South Vietnam with them, along with a dream of sharing those flavors with a new community. We opened Phở Ao Sen to serve the exact food we grew up on: the comforting soups, the fresh herbs, the recipes handed down through generations.

“We've raised prices where we had to. We kept phở and every soup as low as we possibly could — everyone should still be able to eat here.”

Since 2001 we've served the Bay Area with the freshest ingredients we can find. Every dish comes from our kitchen to your table, completely family owned and operated, and every pot of soup is simmered for a full 24 hours.
