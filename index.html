Our story

A lotus that bloomed
in muddy water.

Ao Sen means lotus pond — a Vietnamese symbol of strength and growth. Our family immigrated from Saigon during the Vietnam War and opened this kitchen in 2001 to share three generations of recipes with the Bay Area.

We still simmer every pot of soup for 24 hours or more, roll the herbs fresh, and cook the food we grew up on: comforting phở, grilled meats, rice plates and bánh mì.

Now catering all events. Ask us how.
